    Late-Payment Penalty

    The shop's rate for every credit account

    3% a month late-payment penalty on the goods still unpaid, for every month a credit sale is past its 30-day due date.

    The rate could not be read. Reload the page to try again.

    A sale is charged the day after its due date and again every thirty days it stays unpaid, at the rate in force that day; a part of a month counts as a month. Changing the rate here does not touch a month already charged. An account given its own rate on its credit terms keeps that rate instead of this one.

    Credit Terms and Conditions

    Printed with every credit account

    The page also prints the store's details, the customer, the credit limit, the days to pay and the late-payment rate from the account itself, then these terms, then the signature lines. Do not type those figures here: they would go out of date.
